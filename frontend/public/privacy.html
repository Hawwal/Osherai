Osher AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Osher AI uses profile, savings goal, wallet session, and activity data to provide savings plans, AI coaching, reminders, and transaction status updates.

Data collected

  • Account details such as name, email, or phone when provided.
  • Wallet session details, including wallet type and public wallet reference.
  • Savings goals, deposits, withdrawals, round-up logs, tips, and AI chat history.

How data is used

Data is used to operate the app, personalize coaching, keep goal progress in sync, and provide support.

Wallet keys

Osher AI never stores private keys or recovery phrases.
